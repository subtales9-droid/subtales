Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the SubTales platform and its integrations.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SubTales ("SubTales", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our website, content automation workflows, and integrations with third-party social media platforms.

By using SubTales, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About SubTales

SubTales is a content creation and automation platform designed to generate storytelling videos and assist with publishing content to social media platforms.

SubTales may integrate with third-party platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and X to allow authorized users to publish or manage content through their connected accounts.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use SubTales and which integrations you authorize, the platform may process the following types of information:

  • Basic account information provided by connected social media platforms.
  • Social media account identifiers required to identify an authorized account.
  • Authentication and authorization information required to connect third-party services.
  • Content created or submitted through the SubTales workflow, including stories, captions, titles, hashtags, and generated videos.
  • Technical information required to operate and troubleshoot the platform.

3. Social Media Integrations

SubTales can connect with third-party social media services when the user explicitly authorizes the connection.

These integrations may include:

  • TikTok
  • YouTube
  • Instagram
  • X

When you authorize an integration, SubTales only uses the permissions granted by you and the capabilities made available by the respective platform.

Each third-party platform has its own privacy policy and terms of service. We recommend reviewing those policies before authorizing an integration.

4. TikTok Integration

If you connect your TikTok account to SubTales, the integration may process information required to authenticate your account and publish content on your behalf.

Depending on the permissions granted by the user and approved by TikTok, this may include basic account information and authorization information associated with the TikTok account.

SubTales may use the TikTok Content Posting API to send generated videos to the TikTok account authorized by the user.

SubTales does not request or store the user's TikTok password.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by SubTales may be used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and connect authorized social media accounts.
  • To generate, prepare, and publish content requested by the user.
  • To provide the functionality of the SubTales platform.
  • To maintain and improve the platform.
  • To diagnose technical problems and errors.
  • To protect the platform against unauthorized access or misuse.

6. Content and Generated Videos

SubTales may process content supplied by the user or generated through the platform, including stories, scripts, captions, hashtags, audio, images, and videos.

Generated content is processed for the purpose of creating and publishing the content requested by the user.

Users are responsible for ensuring that the content they create or publish complies with applicable laws and the rules of the social media platforms they use.

7. Data Sharing

SubTales does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms when necessary to provide an integration explicitly authorized by the user.

For example, when a user authorizes TikTok publishing, the content required for the publishing operation may be transmitted to TikTok through the applicable TikTok API.

SubTales may also use infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate the platform, host its services, process content, or maintain technical functionality.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by SubTales against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid sharing passwords, access tokens, API secrets, or other confidential credentials with unauthorized individuals.

9. Authentication Credentials

SubTales does not request users to provide their social media passwords directly.

Authentication with supported platforms is performed through the authorization mechanisms provided by the respective platform.

Access permissions can be revoked by the user through the applicable third-party platform settings or authorization controls.

10. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain the service, resolve technical issues, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of SubTales, subject to applicable legal or technical requirements.

11. Third-Party Services

SubTales may interact with third-party services, including social media platforms and infrastructure providers.

These services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, terms, data retention practices, and security measures.

SubTales is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that operate independently from the SubTales platform.

12. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights regarding their personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing activities.

Users may also revoke previously granted permissions for connected social media accounts.

13. Children's Privacy

SubTales is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to SubTales without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the SubTales platform, integrations, legal requirements, or operational practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by SubTales, you can contact the SubTales project through its official project repository.

16. Acceptance

By using SubTales or authorizing an integration with a supported third-party platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
